# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- On a release (or a manual run with a topic and notes or a page), plans and writes an article in the brand's
  voice from the release notes, then drafts posts for the platforms you name from that article.
- Leaves everything in Skryvo for approval; publishes and schedules nothing.
- Outputs the plan and article ids, the article's link in Skryvo and the drafted posts as JSON, and writes a job
  summary.
